Writeup

Our goal with this EV Market Dataset was to create a visualization that reflects the price trends and impact of inflation on EV vehicles from 2020-2026 (available year range). We decided that the best way to do this would be through line graphs to show the price trend across the years. To analyze across brands, we added dropdown filters to compare brands against each other as well as the tiers (from high to low: ‘Luxury’, ‘Mid-range’, ‘Premium’, ‘Budget’).

Initial exploratory data analysis was done by Lucia, Neeraja, and Gloria, creating visualizations to gain an understanding of what the dataset contained and represented. We created a scatterplot to show the pricing of different brands across each tier, a line graph to show EV price by brand over time, and more. A correlation analysis heatmap with price is what drove our question to see how brands have reacted to price inflation. After deciding our research question, Benjamin cleaned the data so that it can be used in the final interactive visualization. The cleaning process consisted of creating a new csv file where the only variables kept were; brand, market_segment, model, year, price_usd. Using this data we were able to find the average price of a brand for every year at a specific tier.

When creating the initial visualizations we noticed an issue with brand lines overlapping and tiers having the same color. To handle the large range of brands in the dataset, we hard-coded colors for each brand to avoid confusion. Additionally, to handle the different tiers we assigned tiers a line graph styling (i.e dashed, solid, dot dash). Another interesting design choice was not including axis labels; dollar signs ($) are written into every y-axis tick, and years is a common x-axis, so we considered axis labels unnecessary "ink".

For the viewer interaction, we chose to use a dropdown feature with checkboxes to indicate to viewers that they can select multiple brands/tiers to compare the electric vehicle prices. To further emphasize the price differences and allow for easier comparison, we added tooltip for each year, showing the average price of the selected brand and/or tier at that year. The legend was moved from traditional top right to its own column on the right so that when adding multiple brands/tiers the legend would not overlap with the visualization. Lastly, we included a dynamic y-axis that changes with the applied filters. The filters are also able to be toggled on and off to allow the viewer an easier, less cluttered viewing experience.
